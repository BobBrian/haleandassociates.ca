CORPORATE SERVICE

Hale & Associates offers a wide spectrum of services to the corporate community. Today’s corporate environment is ever changing. Knowing who you are doing business with is as important as who you are in business with. Our corporate investigators come from diversified corporate backgrounds offering a facet of experience, knowledge and substantial resources and business contacts to draw from. The corporate specialists at Hale & Associates have been investigating corporate fraud for over a decade with a proven track record of results in all areas of corporate investigations. We understand that a successful investigation is not only about followinga subject and obtaining quality video. It is about research and gathering facts and information as well as preparing a detailed factual report that you and your legal counsel can be confident in.
