LEGAL SERVICES

Hale & Associates provides litigation support to solicitors to identify and investigate Civil, Criminal, Defense, and Labour cases. We supply a complete and comprehensive awareness of all pertinent details to assist in case preparation.

We will conduct surveillance, complete thorough background investigations, revealing relevant information on a subject’s personal, professional, financial, and criminal both past and present. We identify, locate and interview witnesses. We will also conduct formal interviews of the witnesses and or other parties involved and record statements in their entirety, whether written, audio, or audio/visual.

We are able to obtain and validate evidence and documents you need that are relevant or even paramount to your case. The combination of ourresearch services, interview/interrogation techniques, and a highly developed ability to penetrate and cooperate with, even the toughest of neighbourhoods, allows us to obtain decisive and often vital results in criminal circumstances.

Civil and family court investigations require unqualified care and delicacy, skills at which Hale & Associates team members excel.
