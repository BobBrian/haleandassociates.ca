Careers at Hale & Associates

As we continue to grow and expand, Hale & Associates is committed to evolving with the latest technological advancements in the investigation industry. Our staffing needs in both investigation and administration departments are regularly updated to meet these changes. Our Human Resources Department frequently updates our website with current employment opportunities.

If there are no available positions at the time of your visit, we still encourage interested applicants to forward their resumes via fax or email to the attention of our Human Resources Department. Please refer to the contact section for appropriate details.

Hale & Associates is an equal opportunity employer and adheres to the policies outlined by the Employment Standards Act of Ontario. Applicants will be selected for interviews only if they are best suited for the available positions or specific investigative operations. Due to the volume of inquiries, we regret that we are unable to respond to every application.

Thank you for your interest in potential employment with Hale & Associates Investigation Services.

“Investigate, don’t speculate!”

Current Positions

Surveillance Investigator

We are currently accepting resumes for experienced Surveillance Investigators familiar with insurance and domestic investigations. Positions are available in the City of Kawartha Lakes area and the Markham/Toronto area.

Investigators must supply their own suitable vehicle and video/photographic equipment. Specialized equipment required for certain assignments will be provided by the firm.

We offer an above-average remuneration package and an excellent working environment equipped with the latest technology. Only candidates meeting our requirements will be considered and contacted.

Please forward your resume, along with a cover letter and your salary expectations, to info@haleandassociates.ca for consideration.
