Privacy Statement

Introduction

Hale & Associates Investigation Services Ltd is a provincially based investigative firm headquartered in Lindsay, Ontario, conducting investigations throughout Ontario. We collect personal information daily and recognize the critical importance of protecting, storing, and securely disposing of this information.

2. Identifying Purposes

We clearly identify the purposes for collecting personal information before or at the time of collection. Any new purposes arising during an investigation will require obtaining new consent when necessary.

3. Consent

We obtain appropriate consent for the collection, use, or disclosure of personal information, except where permitted by law. Consent may be express or implied depending on the situation.

4. Limiting Collection

We only collect information necessary for specific, legitimate purposes, using fair and lawful means without deception.

5. Limiting Use, Disclosure, and Retention

Personal information is only used or disclosed for the purposes for which it was collected, unless otherwise authorized by law. Information is retained only as long as necessary and then securely destroyed or anonymized.

6. Accuracy

We ensure that personal information is accurate, complete, and up-to-date as necessary. Clients help verify accuracy during initial collection and review stages.

7. Safeguards

We employ appropriate security measures based on the sensitivity of information, including physical, administrative, and technological safeguards. Sensitive electronic files are encrypted before transfer, and secure methods are used for disposal.

8. Openness

We provide easily understandable information about our privacy policies to the public, either in print or online at www.haleandassociates.ca.

9. Individual Access

Individuals may request access to their personal information held by us, subject to lawful exceptions such as protecting others' privacy, solicitor-client privilege, or investigative reasons.

10. Challenging Compliance

Challenges regarding our compliance with privacy policies may be directed to our Chief Privacy Officer listed above.
