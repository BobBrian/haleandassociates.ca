Consultation

Hale & Associates Investigation Services offers no-charge consultation to all prospective clients, whether you are a solicitor, corporate representative, member of the insurance community, or a private individual. We strongly believe that anyone requiring investigation services should be fully informed and receive immediate advice and action necessary to initiate their investigation efficiently.

Through consultation with our executive team, we will provide you with personal and discreet attention to your situation, understanding that most investigations are of a sensitive nature. During the consultation, we will outline the most cost-effective and efficient plan of action to achieve your goals.

Hale & Associates will work to provide an accurate cost analysis for each case, ensuring we operate within your financial boundaries. Our investigators are highly trained, efficient, and possess strong time management skills to deliver effective results.

Our diversity and flexibility allow us to adapt to the unique characteristics of each situation. We maintain ongoing communication with our clients throughout the investigation process, ensuring the most proficient outcome.

Our client base is wide and diversified, including Fortune 500 companies, financial institutions, insurance organizations, highly recognized law firms, government agencies, and private individuals. We also provide services to medium-sized businesses and smaller operations that may not have internal investigative resources.

Hale & Associates is committed to serving a broad spectrum of businesses with clients that are multi-faceted and diversified in scope.

A member of our staff is available around the clock, seven days a week, to consult with you regarding your investigative needs. We accept rush and time-sensitive investigations that require immediate attention.
