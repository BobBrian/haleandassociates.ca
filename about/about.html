About Us

Hale & Associates is a progressive, full-service investigation firm, providing services to the corporate, legal, and insurance communities, as well as private individuals regarding their domestic and family service needs.

We are a premier Ontario investigation firm. With our industry associations and affiliates throughout North America and over thirty countries worldwide, Hale & Associates can provide investigative assistance to clients almost anywhere in the world.

We employ a highly experienced and diversified staff with backgrounds ranging from banking and policing to human resources and computer specialties. Our team of specialized investigators is trained in surveillance, field interviews, document retrieval, and expert testimony. Each case is assigned to an investigator specializing in the relevant area to best suit our client's needs. Our staff is committed to continuous education to ensure we provide the best service possible.

Our background and pre-employment screening processes are conducted by investigators who have specialized training in human resource analysis, criminal research, and civil document retrieval.

To complement our skilled staff, we utilize the latest and most advanced investigative technology available today.

Hale & Associates has built a strong reputation across Ontario as a leader in confidential civil and criminal investigations, known for providing powerful evidence in undercover operations.

Our expertise has been recognized throughout the legal and business communities for delivering the most informative and comprehensive investigative services in the industry. We thoroughly examine, analyze, and investigate a wide range of resources to help clients make effective business decisions and mitigate risk. We have the experience and background necessary to handle even the most complex investigations.

Our goal is to provide clients with a timely, comprehensive, cost-effective, and professional investigative approach tailored to achieving their objectives.

Every investigation is conducted meticulously, with documentation and client communication as a top priority. Client satisfaction is always our primary focus. Hale & Associates is licensed under the Ministry of Community Safety and Correctional Services, bonded, and insured.
